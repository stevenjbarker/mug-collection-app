        What actually happened

          Real history: yearly returns for savings (3-month T-bills), the S&P 500, U.S. home prices and gold from Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern. Credit card rates for late bills from the Federal Reserve (G.19, and its 2014 report to Congress for years before late 1994). CD and government bond rates from the Federal Reserve (H.15): the 6-month CD rate through mid-2013, then the 1-year Treasury yield; 1-month, 3-month and 1-year Treasury yields (the 1-month uses the 3-month before 2001). Monthly stock prices from Yahoo Finance, adjusted for dividends and splits.
